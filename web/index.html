Every OAuth coding plan, and what a token actually costs on it. Most vendors publish a price and a multiplier and never the number in between, so this tracks two things separately: what they publish, and what people measure.

What the columns mean

Credit multiple is dollars of usage included divided by dollars paid. Cursor Ultra at $200 including $400 of usage is 2.0x. It is exact, published, and directly comparable, but only exists for plans denominated in dollars.

$/Mtok is the subscription price divided by the tokens it actually buys. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and xAI publish no token figure at all, so this stays blank until somebody measures it. Run node probe/probe.js to measure your own.

Basis is where the number came from. published means the vendor states it, derived means it was computed from something they state, measured means it came from real session logs, unknown means nobody has a defensible figure yet.

Measure your own plan 
node probe/probe.js --days 30 --plan claude-max-20x

Reads the token counts Claude Code and Codex already write to ~/.claude/projects and ~/.codex/sessions, prices them at that vendor's own list API rate, and divides by what you pay. Nothing leaves your machine unless you pass --submit.

Data lives in data/providers/*.json. Every plan carries a source URL and a checked date. Corrections by pull request.
